NES 2.0 Mapper 530

NES 2.0 Mapper 530

NES 2.0 Mapper 530 is used for Super Mario Bros. Pocker Mali (sic), a bootleg version of Bandai's Crayon Shin-chan: Ora to Poi Poi. It's a VRC4 clone (A0/A1, VRC4f) with the PRG and CHR address lines scrambled: PRG A14 is swapped with PRG A16, and CHR A15 is swapped with CHR A16. Its UNIF board name is UNL-AX5705.
